## Primary result

R. Arratia, L. Goldstein and L. Gordon, *Two moments suffice for
Poisson approximations: the Chen--Stein method*, Annals of Probability 17
(1989), 9--25, DOI [10.1214/aop/1176991491](https://doi.org/10.1214/aop/1176991491).

The definitions of the neighborhoods and of `b1`, `b2`, and `b3` begin on
printed page 10.  The total-variation convention and Theorem 1 are on printed
page 11.  The repeated `p. 10` locator in the frozen Lean comments is therefore
one page early for the theorem itself.

For a Bernoulli family `(X_alpha)`, with `p_alpha = E X_alpha`,
`W = sum X_alpha`, and `lambda = E W`, Theorem 1 bounds the distance between
the law of `W` and `Poisson(lambda)` in terms of `b1 + b2 + b3`.  Its displayed
coarse consequence is at most `2 * (b1 + b2 + b3)` in the source's
total-variation norm.

## Why `b3 = 0`

`HasExactDependencyGraph` says that, for every `alpha`, every Boolean value,
and every complete Boolean pattern outside `B_alpha`, the joint atom factors
as the product of its two marginal atoms.  On the finite outside-vector
space, arbitrary events are disjoint unions of such atoms.  Thus `X_alpha` is
independent of the sigma-algebra generated by the complete outside vector.
Consequently the conditional expectation of `X_alpha - p_alpha` given that
sigma-algebra is zero (zero-mass atoms do not contribute), which is exactly
the vanishing of the source term `b3`.

## Zero marginals and zero intensity

The source theorem is stated for positive marginals.  The Challenge also
allows degenerate coordinates, so two reductions are needed.

If `lambda = sum_alpha p_alpha = 0`, nonnegativity gives `p_alpha = 0` for
every `alpha`.  Each indicator is then false with probability one, `W = 0`
almost surely, and `Poisson(0)` is the point mass at zero.  The Challenge
total variation is therefore zero, while its right-hand side is nonnegative.

If `lambda > 0` but some `p_alpha` vanish, restrict the family to the indices
with positive marginal.  A zero-marginal Boolean indicator is false almost
surely, so this restriction does not change the law of `W` or its Poisson
parameter.  Exact dependency passes to the restricted graph.  Every removed
`b1` term contains a zero marginal, and every removed `b2` term contains a
joint event contained in a zero-marginal event; those terms are zero.  Thus
the restricted `b1` and `b2` are no larger than the original quantities, and
the source theorem applies to the remaining positive-marginal family.

## Total-variation normalization and constant

The Challenge uses half of the `l1` distance between the two probability
masses.  For discrete probability laws this is the standard supremum-over-
events distance.  The AGG paper's norm is the unhalved variation norm.  After
setting `b3 = 0`, the source coarse bound therefore implies the stronger
standard-TV estimate

```text
Challenge total variation <= b1 + b2.
```

The registered Lean proposition only asks for

```text
Challenge total variation <= 2 * (b1 + b2).
```

Since both sums are nonnegative, the Challenge inequality is a conservative
consequence.  It is not an exact transcription of the sharp source constant.
