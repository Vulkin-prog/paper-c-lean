\section*{Declaration on artificial-intelligence assistance}
\phantomsection\label{sec:ai-assistance}
The research was organized and directed by the author with substantial
assistance from language-model systems, including Claude Fable 5 and
Claude Opus 4.8 (Anthropic), and GPT-5.6 Sol and GPT-6 Astra (OpenAI).
These systems were used to explore candidate lemmas and proof routes,
draft and reorganize manuscripts, assist with the separate Lean development,
write diagnostic programs, check references and build artifacts, and
conduct adversarial reviews through separate instances.

GPT-6 Astra assisted in particular with the selected-pivot and Palm
arguments, the empirical corollary, the dyadic restriction and local-field
obstruction, and the exposition, bibliography and preparation of this
edition. This attribution preserves the other models' historical
contributions. The author determines the claims and scope and accepts
responsibility for the arguments, references and any remaining errors.
AI-assisted checks are not independent human peer review. The
mathematical claims rest on the printed arguments and stated external
results, not on model output or the success of finite diagnostic tests.
