\section*{Formalization and availability}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Formalization and availability}
This article and its technical companion, both Version 3PREL9, form one
proof package. The sources, frozen bibliographic data, build instructions
and finite diagnostic programs accompany this edition, dated
20 September 2026. The proofs and their literature inputs are given in
the two manuscripts; finite diagnostics do not replace asymptotic arguments.

The associated \href{https://github.com/Vulkin-prog/paper-c-lean}{Lean development}
is separate from the written proof and is not an input to it.
The accompanying formalization correspondence records completed mathematical
realignment with Version 3PREL8, relative to explicit literature premises
and recorded proof substitutions. It covers the typical and affine
dictionaries, information-adapted and constrained cutoffs, microscopic
signed field, dyadic restriction and common readouts, empirical count law
and its support obstruction, and Palm/cumulant complements. Preserved
results retain the earlier correspondence and its stated fidelity limits.
This edition incorporates the documented monotonicity substitutions and
local proof clarifications without changing the conclusions.

The formal development retains seven explicit literature propositions:
scalar Stein factors, process Chen--Stein comparison, the prime number
theorem, directional Stein factors, the uniform Laishram--Shorey estimate,
the Shorey square-product exclusion, and the Nicolas--Robin divisor bound.
They are theorem arguments, not internally proved consequences of an axiom
audit. In particular, the analytic construction of the Stein solution in
\cref{supp:pivot:lem:palm} remains a premise of that formal proof; its printed
semigroup construction is not reproduced there. The finite coupling
argument is checked relative to this input. The elementary support
obstruction in \cref{rem:empirical-field-obstruction} uses none of these
literature premises.

The supplied completion record is not a new validation receipt for the
source files of this editorial edition. No Lean build was run in preparing
Version 3PREL9, and no new Palomar qualification is claimed. The existing
Palomar configurations and earlier receipts retain their original scope.
The source archive separates the supplied correspondence, the literature
register and the present editorial concordance; it does not replace a
source-bound formal validation record.
