\medskip
Runge's split-product estimate, the Hamming-ball argument, the raw and
capped two-window profiles, the simple-incidence rank lemma and the
relative bulk normalization are proved in the article--companion pair.
Evertse--Silverman is contextual only. The selected-pivot comparison and
Palm complements are proved in Appendices F--G, with the latter using,
not establishing, the microscopic comparison. The table concerns the
mathematical inputs to the written arguments. In particular the directional estimate is obtained
directly from Barbour's two lemmas, while the arithmetic comparison and
Poisson filling are proved here.


\paragraph{Boundary of the formal correspondence.}
This table describes the written proof, not a list of assumptions all
proved inside Lean. The separate literature register supplies seven
explicit propositions to the formal development, as listed in the
article's formalization statement. In particular, the directional input
supplies the entrywise bound and the weighted quadratic bound for the
\emph{same} Stein solution. An entrywise bound by one is not an
unweighted Euclidean operator-norm bound. The derivation in
\cref{supp:eq:directional-factor} uses precisely the valid weighted and
entrywise estimates; no arithmetic bound changes. The finite Palm
argument in \cref{supp:pivot:lem:palm} is formalized relative to the
analytic solution input, rather than by reconstructing its printed
semigroup. The correspondence distinguishes this boundary from the
proved arithmetic deductions, proof substitutions and release qualification.
