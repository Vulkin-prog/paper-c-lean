\paragraph{Restriction to a dyadic block.}
The one-factor hard-field budget is not confined to prefix geometry.
Restricting a larger prefix retains the original integer start labels.

\begin{dyadiccorollary}[Dyadic signed field at the one-factor budget]
\label{cor:dyadic-micro}
Let $L_N=\lfloor\log_2N\rfloor-d_N$, where $d_N\in\NN$ and
$d_N=o(\log N)$, and put $\Lambda_N=N2^{-L_N}\to\infty$.
Suppose $C_N\in\mathcal F_{Y_N^\sharp}$ has positive probability and,
for a fixed $c>0$,
\[
 I_N^{\rm info}:=-\log\PP(C_N),\qquad
 I_N^{\rm info}+\log\Lambda_N\le V_N-c\nu_N.
\]
For $0<c'<c$ and $0<\varepsilon<1/3$, with
$I_N=[N,2N)\cap\Z$,
\[
 \dTV\!\left(
  \Law((K^s_{x,e})_{x\in I_N,e\ge0,s=\pm1}\mid C_N),
  \bigotimes_{x\in I_N,e\ge0,s=\pm1}\Pois(2^{-L_N-e-2})
 \right)
 \ll_{c,c',\varepsilon}e^{-c'\nu_N}+N^{-1/3+\varepsilon}.
\]
The coordinates retain the full exact excess and actual sign at every
start; the target uses the same integer labels.
\end{dyadiccorollary}
\begin{proof}
Take $M=4N$ and keep the same length $L_N$. Then
\[
 \lfloor\log_2M\rfloor-L_N=d_N+2=o(\log M),\qquad
 (M-L_N)2^{-L_N}=(4-L_N/N)\Lambda_N.
\]
Every base starting in $I_N$ is contained in $[1,M]$ for large $N$;
no restriction is imposed on its full rightward length.

We record the stability of the hard scales, including the conditioning
field. Write $H=\log t$ and $v(H)=V_t$, so
$v=D(H/v)$. Since $D'(z)=u(z)>0$, implicit differentiation gives
\[
 v'(H)=\frac{u(H/v)}{v+(H/v)u(H/v)}>0,
 \qquad v'(H)=O(1/\nu_t).
\]
The latter estimate follows from $v\sim\nu_tu(\nu_t)$ and
$\nu_t=H/v$. Consequently
\[
 V_{4N}-V_N=O(1/\nu_N)=o(\nu_N),\qquad
 \nu_{4N}\sim\nu_N,\qquad Y_{4N}^\sharp\ge Y_N^\sharp.
\]
In particular $C_N$ belongs to the hard field at $M=4N$. Choose
$c'<b<a<c$. The bounded change in log intensity and the displayed
scale estimates imply
\[
 I_N^{\rm info}+\log\big((4N-L_N)2^{-L_N}\big)
       \le V_{4N}-a\nu_{4N}
\]
for all sufficiently large $N$. Apply \cref{thm:micro-run-tv} at $4N$
with margin $a$ and rate $b$, then project onto $x\in I_N$.
Total variation contracts under this projection, and eventually
$e^{-b\nu_{4N}}\le e^{-c'\nu_N}$. The coordinate means are unchanged.
\end{proof}
The passage is a restriction of integer coordinates, not an approximate
identification of two rescaled grids. It improves the available dyadic
hard-field range at growing intensity. It neither enlarges the permitted
prime sigma-field nor proves a comparison for arbitrary dictionaries;
these require their own hypotheses and estimates.
