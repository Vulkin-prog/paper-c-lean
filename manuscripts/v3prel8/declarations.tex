\section*{Formalization and availability}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Formalization and availability}
This article and its technical companion, both Version 3PREL8, form one
proof package. The sources, frozen bibliographic data, build instructions
and finite diagnostic programs accompany this edition, dated
16 September 2026. The proofs and their literature inputs are given in
the two manuscripts; finite diagnostics do not replace asymptotic arguments.

The associated \href{https://github.com/Vulkin-prog/paper-c-lean}{Lean development}
is separate from the written proof and is not an input to it.
No formal certification of this edition and no new Lean build are
reported. In particular, the typical-dictionary and information-adapted
cutoff results, selected-pivot and Palm/cumulant developments, empirical
corollary, dyadic restriction and local-field obstruction are not
represented as certified by the historical development. A Lean proof
under external hypotheses does not establish those hypotheses.
