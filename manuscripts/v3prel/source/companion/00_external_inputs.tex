\section*{External inputs and internal deductions}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{External inputs and internal deductions}
This register identifies the non-elementary inputs, their precise role,
and the calculations proved in this package. References are local to this
companion; statement links lead to the relevant document. Classical
finite-dimensional linear algebra, conditioning and elementary inequalities
are not separate imported hypotheses.

\medskip
Runge's split-product estimate, the Hamming-ball argument, the raw and
capped two-window profiles, the simple-incidence rank lemma and the
relative bulk normalization are proved in the article--companion pair.
Evertse--Silverman is contextual only; the separate process article is
not a proof dependency. The table concerns the mathematical inputs to the
written arguments. In particular the directional estimate is obtained
directly from Barbour's two lemmas, while the arithmetic comparison and
Poisson filling are proved here.
