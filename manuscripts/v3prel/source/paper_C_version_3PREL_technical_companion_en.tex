\begin{abstract}
This companion records the detailed inputs and verifications used in the
article. The square-product argument is given once, in the article, in a
macroscopic geometry that also contains the dyadic and bounded-ratio cases.
Here we verify the growing-degree Runge calculation and the bounded-height
Pell count, the prime-cutoff estimates, scalar smoothing, aggregated and labelled marked comparisons, and conditional-kernel consequences. The boundary verification uses a
simple incidence graph and a global count of square divisibilities to obtain
a rank surplus $28271/332640$ and the displayed relative exponent $1/12$.
The signed marked-field comparison retains the sign of each run. The
article's capped value-relation estimate yields joint laws for growing
families of prescribed words; this companion records the marginal-ceiling
calculation, local ranks and tail estimates used in those transfers. Appendix letters refer to this companion;
numbered sections and unqualified numbered statements refer to the article.
\end{abstract}
