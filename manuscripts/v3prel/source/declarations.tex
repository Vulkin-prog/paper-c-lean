\section*{Formalization and availability}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Formalization and availability}
The mathematical proofs are given in this article and its technical
companion, with the literature inputs identified where they are used.
An associated Lean development is available at
\href{https://github.com/Vulkin-prog/paper-c-lean}{Vulkin-prog/paper-c-lean}.
It is separate from the written proof and is not used as an input to it.
No formal certification of the present article or companion is claimed.
Checking a Lean proof under external hypotheses does not establish the
truth of those hypotheses.

The article and technical companion form one research object associated
with Zenodo concept DOI
\href{https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21736676}{10.5281/zenodo.21736676}
and Cambridge Open Engage record
\href{https://doi.org/10.33774/coe-2026-z3l74}{10.33774/coe-2026-z3l74}.
The source archive contains the LaTeX files, compilation instructions,
finite diagnostic programs and a file manifest. No version-specific DOI
is assigned here. Finite diagnostics detect algebraic and implementation
errors; they do not replace the proofs of the asymptotic results.
